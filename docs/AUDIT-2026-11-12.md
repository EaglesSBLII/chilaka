# Full audit, 12 November 2026

Five independent reviewers went through the app: the lesson loop, games and progress,
accounts and sync, all Telugu content, and every recording of both voices. This file lists
what was fixed, what still needs a person's ears or a decision, and how to check it.

An audit like this lowers the number of bugs. It cannot prove there are none, and nothing
here replaces listening to the recordings.

## Fixed

**Reading & Writing**
- Letters first: units 1 to 6 teach single letters only, each card says just the letter.
  Words start in "First words"; vowel-sign units teach the syllables before the words.
- Two letters no longer share a label: ఈ is "ii", ఊ is "uu", ఏ is "ee", ఓ is "oo", matching
  the spelling the rest of the app uses.
- Letter meanings are short and uniform, so the longest option is no longer the answer.
- Review rows only appear beside other rows in multiple choice.
- The item "క క్క" was removed: its recording said a different word than its text.
- A typing exercise can be skipped when the phone has no Telugu keyboard.
- Wrong-answer banners show the Telugu letter, not a romanisation.
- 13 notes that became false when words moved were rewritten.

**Lessons (both tracks)**
- Check is greyed out until there is an answer.
- The progress bar no longer slides backwards after a mistake.
- A failed save at the end of a lesson no longer leaves a dead screen.
- Stopping the microphone while the scorer is still loading no longer strands the exercise.
- Every letter of a lesson is now written at least once.

**Progress, coins and games**
- A bought streak freeze was wiped by the next sync while the coins stayed spent. Freezes are
  now worked out from the synced history, so they cannot be lost or doubled.
- The Play tab's "due" badge no longer sticks on items the games cannot deal.
- The path remembers whether you were on Speaking or Read & Write.
- Games no longer show the answer next to a Telugu letter.
- Daily-goal and streak-milestone coins are paid when a game earns them, not only lessons.
- A game round where nothing was solved no longer counts toward the streak or quests.
- A streak that is already broken cannot be repaired by buying freezes afterwards.
- The day now rolls over at midnight where the phone is.

**Accounts, sync and the dashboard**
- Sync uses one slot for a whole attempt, so signing in mid-sync cannot mix two slots.
- Accounts are switched off in the developer version, so test progress never reaches a real
  account.
- Dashboard sign-in keeps working when Cloudflare's daily storage quota is used up.
- The web app no longer deploys if the server's tests fail.

**Speaking track content (meanings only; no recording was changed)**
- Four meanings corrected (u04-i17, u39-i02, u50-i15, u55-i04), grandmother spelling made
  consistent in three notes, six answer options that pointed at another unit removed.

## Needs your ears

The letter recordings were cut down from "letter, then a word" to the letter alone. The cut
was checked by machine, not heard. Listen to these first.

- **Female:** ఛ, శ, ఊ, ఢ, కీ, ఖ, ప, ట, కా, ఠ, కై, కొ, కూ, క్ష, ఈ, కి; words చకచక, హలం, పేరు.
- **Male:** మనం, క, ఠ, ఢ, కా, కి, ఖ, కె, కొ, కౌ, క్ష, కు, ఔ, ఈ, ఫ, ఝ, అ; words చిలక, రైలు.
- **Long and short vowels:** in most pairs (ఇ/ఈ, ఎ/ఏ, ఒ/ఓ) the long vowel is not audibly
  longer than the short one. This was already true of the original recordings. Fixing it
  means recording the long vowels again and choosing takes by ear.
- Full measurements are in the audit's scratch folder; the summary is `audit-summary.json`.

## Needs a decision

1. **Signing out keeps the phone attached to the account.** If someone else then signs in on
   that phone, the first person's progress merges into their account and cannot be undone.
   Options: always detach on sign-out, or ask before merging.
2. **The sync code is the real key.** Reading or writing a slot needs only its code, so a
   password reset does not lock anyone out who has it, and exported backups contain it.
3. **Settings do not sync between devices**, and two devices keep re-uploading because each
   thinks its settings are newer.
4. **The visit counter writes to Cloudflare on every app open**, with no limit. Heavy traffic
   could use up the free daily quota that sync also depends on.
5. **Mac updater:** if copying the new app fails halfway, it can leave a partial app.
6. **Telugu to check with a native speaker:** u60-i01 (double "to" ending), u53-i02 (a
   Hyderabad slang word that may be a swear word), u62-i15 (tense), u57 dialogue (Kiran
   switches between casual and polite). Changing any of these means recording them again.
7. **The website has no Mac download link**; the flyers send Mac users to GitHub.

## How it was checked

- Unit tests: 807 pass.
- End-to-end tests: see the release notes for the final run.
- Every lesson of both tracks was generated with 30 to 40 different shuffles and checked for
  impossible or duplicate answers.
- All 1,204 recordings in each voice: none missing, none silent, none clipped.
